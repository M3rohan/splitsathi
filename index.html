SplitMitra Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

SplitMitra is developed by Rohan Mohite. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, used to create your account and to let group members find you.
  • Expense data: groups, expenses, amounts, categories and splits that you enter.
  • Notification data: in-app notifications created when group members add expenses.
  • Device and usage data: a device messaging token, app usage events and crash reports, collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve the app.

How we use data

To provide the app's features (groups, balances, settle-up suggestions, notifications), to keep your account secure, and to find and fix bugs.

Who can see your data

Members of a group can see that group's name, its members and its expenses. We do not sell your data. Data is stored and processed using Google Firebase services, which act as our service provider.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules, so that only group members can read a group's data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, then Delete Account. This deletes your profile and removes you from groups you joined. Groups you created, and expense records inside shared groups, may remain so that other members' balances stay accurate. To request removal of specific data, email rohanmohite6785@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
